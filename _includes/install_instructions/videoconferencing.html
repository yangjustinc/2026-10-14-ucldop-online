Microsoft Teams

The workshop will be delivered using Microsoft Teams. UCL staff and students should use the UCL Information Services Division guidance for Microsoft Teams for information about accessing Teams, signing in, meetings, and accessibility features.

Joining instructions will be sent to registered participants before the workshop. Before your first session, please check that you can sign in with your UCL account and join a Teams meeting on the computer you will use for the workshop, and that your audio is working. Headphones or a headset are recommended, particularly if you are joining from one of the shared rooms at Maple House.

Set up your workspace

Carpentries workshops are taught by live coding, so you will need to see both Teams and the software you are working in. We recommend one of the following arrangements:

  • Two monitors: use one monitor for Teams and the other for your coding environment.
  • Two devices: use a second device for Teams and your main computer for coding.
  • One screen: arrange Teams and your coding environment side by side, or practise switching between them using keyboard shortcuts.

The Carpentries' guidance on screen layouts for online workshops has additional suggestions.
